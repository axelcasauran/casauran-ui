# Platform Parity

Component and feature parity are insufficient for an enterprise UI product. `registry/platform/` tracks 15 seeded cross-cutting domains from the approved KendoReact documentation baseline, including accessibility, security, data binding, integrations, styling, server capabilities, i18n/date math, migration/troubleshooting and AI tooling.

Platform entries advance through evidence independently from individual components. A product release cannot claim broad parity while cross-cutting platform domains remain unreviewed.
